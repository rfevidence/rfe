About us

A leading consulting firm in monitoring and evaluation

Right Fit Evidence (RFE) is a consulting company established in 2019 and based in Maseru, Lesotho. We operate at both national and international levels, providing monitoring and evaluation services to private sector companies, non-profit organisations and government departments.

Our M&E, research and learning expertise helps clients see what is really working, so their programmes move beyond impressions to impact.

Our mission

To provide evidence-based data solutions that facilitate meaningful sustainable planning systems and impact measurement.

Right Fit Evidence Pty Ltd ("RFE", "we", "us") is a monitoring, evaluation, research and learning consultancy based in Maseru, Lesotho. Our work involves collecting and analysing information, sometimes about people, so we take the protection of personal information seriously.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. It applies to this website, rfe.maclesotho.com, to your dealings with us as a client or contact, and to the personal information we collect during research and evaluation assignments.

Our commitment We never sell personal information. Research participants take part voluntarily and with informed consent, and we report findings in a way that does not identify individuals unless they have agreed to it.

3. Research and survey participants

Much of our work is data collection: baseline and endline surveys, evaluations, focus group discussions, key informant interviews, polls and satisfaction surveys. If you take part in one of these, we may collect:

  • Your responses to the questions asked
  • Basic details such as age group, sex, location and household information, where the study needs them
  • Audio recordings, photographs or GPS locations, only where the study requires them and you have agreed
Sensitive topics Some studies touch on health, disability, gender, refugee status or human rights. We collect sensitive information only when it is essential to the study, only with your explicit consent, and we hold it under stricter access controls.

How we handle participant information:

  • Informed consent. Before you take part, we explain the purpose of the study, who it is for, and how your information will be used. Taking part is voluntary, and you may skip any question or stop at any time.
  • Working for a client. Most studies are commissioned by a client such as an NGO, UN agency, government department or company. The client usually decides why the data is collected, and we process it on their instructions under a confidentiality agreement.
  • De-identification. We separate names and contact details from responses as early as possible and report findings in aggregate. We do not publish information that identifies you without your permission.
  • Photographs. We use photographs of people on this website or in reports only with their consent.

4. Why we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss how we can support your programme
  • To deliver the services you engage us for, including evaluations, surveys, training and reporting
  • To carry out research and evaluation in line with the study design and the consent participants gave
  • To manage our business, including contracts, invoicing and our professional and legal obligations
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that affects you.

5. Our legal basis

Under Lesotho's Data Protection Act 2011, we process personal information on these grounds:

  • Consent. Research participants give informed consent before taking part, and website visitors consent to non-essential cookies through our cookie banner. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract. We process client and partner information to deliver the services agreed with them.
  • Legal obligation. We keep some records where Lesotho law requires it.

7. Transfers outside Lesotho

We work in Lesotho, Zimbabwe and Uganda, and some of our service providers store data on servers outside Lesotho and outside the SADC region, including in the United States and the European Union. This applies to our website hosting, analytics, cookie-consent provider, email and some data collection and storage tools.

Where we transfer information outside Lesotho, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient protects it to a standard comparable to Lesotho law, and we share only what the service needs.

8. Cookies, analytics and maps

Our website uses cookies, which are small files stored on your device:

  • Necessary cookies keep the site working and remember your cookie choices. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies, through Google Analytics, tell us how many people visit, which pages they read and roughly where they are from. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time through the banner's preferences link, or block cookies in your browser settings.

Our contact section shows an embedded Google Map of our office. Google may collect information about your visit when the map loads, under Google's own privacy policy. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and YouTube take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

9. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work are kept for up to 2 years, then deleted.
  • Client and contract records are kept for 7 years after the engagement ends, for accounting and legal purposes.
  • Research data is kept for the period agreed with the commissioning client. Identifiable participant data is deleted or anonymised once it is no longer needed for the study.
  • Website analytics are retained in aggregated form according to our analytics account settings.

When a retention period ends, we securely delete or anonymise the information.

10. How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on this website, password-protected and encrypted devices for field data collection, access limited to the team members who need it, and confidentiality agreements for all consultants and enumerators.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the Data Protection Act 2011 requires.

11. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2011, you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to any legal duty we have to keep it
  • Object to processing on compelling legitimate grounds
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This stops further processing based on that consent but does not affect what was done before.

To use any of these rights, email rfe@maclesotho.com or call us. We will respond within 30 days and may first ask you to confirm your identity. If you took part in a study commissioned by a client, we may refer your request to that client or handle it with them.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may raise the matter with the Data Protection Commission established under the Data Protection Act 2011.

12. Children

Our website and services are directed at adults. Where a study involves people under 18, we obtain consent from a parent or guardian, seek the young person's own agreement, and follow additional safeguarding procedures. If you believe we hold a child's information without proper consent, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you directly where we reasonably can.
